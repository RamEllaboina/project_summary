\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
With the rapid adoption of cloud computing, ensuring the security, privacy, and integrity of data transmitted over public networks has become a critical challenge. Traditional security frameworks often suffer from high computational overhead and single points of failure. To address these issues, this project introduces a robust framework for secure data transmission over cloud environments utilizing Improved Blockchain Technology combined with the Chinese Remainder Theorem-based RSA (CRT-RSA) algorithm. 

The proposed system leverages blockchain's decentralized and immutable ledger to prevent data tampering, ensure secure access control, and maintain transparent audit trails without relying on a centralized trust authority. To overcome the performance bottlenecks typical of standard cryptographic operations, the CRT-RSA algorithm is implemented for data encryption and decryption. By applying the Chinese Remainder Theorem, the decryption process is significantly accelerated, reducing the computational load and improving the overall efficiency of secure data exchanges. The combination of an enhanced blockchain architecture for integrity and CRT-RSA for rapid, secure encryption provides a highly efficient, scalable, and tamper-proof solution for transmitting sensitive data across cloud platforms.
\\

\textit{Keywords} : Cloud Computing, Data Security, Blockchain Technology, CRT-RSA, Cryptography, Chinese Remainder Theorem, Secure Transmission.
